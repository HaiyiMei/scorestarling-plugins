## What changes between neighbouring grades

- Initial to 1: tempo ceiling 130 to 160; dotted quarter and more 16ths; first accents and slash bars; still no fills beyond short breaks.
- 1 to 2: first time-signature changes, brushes/rutes, open/closed hi-hat, grace-note fills, right-hand work around toms and ride; ad-lib fills start.
- 2 to 3: triplet feels (6/8, 12/8, swung), simple rolls, 16th hi-hat, 16th snare/kick independence; tempo range opens fully.
- 3 to 4: all regular time signatures, ghost notes, 3-against-2, brushes again, syncopation inside fills, 4-bar solos.
- 4 to 5: left-foot hi-hat independence, free triplet/16th syncopation, irregular meters appear, 8-bar solos, Moeller-type motion for fast hi-hat.
- 5 to 6: more irregular meters, double bass-drum pedal as an option, choked hi-hat, left-hand off-beats under 16ths, 12-bar solos.
- 6 to 7: double pedal moves from option to listed kit item, left-hand lead, double-stroke tom rolls, 7/8 and 5/4 in tests, acoustic kit only.
- 7 to 8: complex left-foot patterns, extended solos, hybrid styles, tone variety.

## Arranging implications

- Define the target by ceilings: Initial = quarter/eighth only, 4/4 (2/4, 3/4), 60-130 bpm, hi-hat/snare/kick with only the right foot busy, no syncopation, no improvised fills.
- Keep 16th notes sparse until G1 and make them the norm only from G3; add 16th triplets/sextuplets only from G3-G5 (triplets and shuffles from G3, 12/8 at G3, free syncopation at G5).
- Use 6/8 or 12/8 grooves from G3; reserve 7/8, 5/4, 9/8 for G6+; time-signature changes inside a song from G2 (occasional) and G5 (frequent).
- Fills: G1 short notated 1-bar breaks; G2 add grace-note rudiment fills (flams); G3 simple rolls; G4 syncopated fills and 4-bar solos; G5+ breaks around band hits; solos 8/12/16 bars for G5/G6/G7.
- Limb independence ladder: right foot only (Initial); right hand around kit (G2); snare+kick 16ths (G3); busier (G4); left-foot hi-hat (G5); left-hand off-beats against RH 16ths and double pedal option (G6); double pedal, left-hand lead (G7); left-foot complexity (G8).
- Colour devices to add or strip: cross stick/snares off (Initial), brushes and rutes (G2), mallets (G3), ghost notes (G4), choked hi-hat (G6). Remove these to simplify down.
- To write a song down a level: drop one ceiling item per step (e.g. replace ghost notes with plain backbeat, 16th hi-hat with eighths, double-kick with single kick, odd meter with 4/4).
- Song length guide: Trinity own-choice durations grow from 45 s-2 min (Initial) to 3.5-5 min (G8); RSL piece maxima 1:00 (Debut) to 3:45 (G8).

## Confidence and gaps

- Time signatures, note values, dynamics, tempo, kit/beaters, independence, technique, duration, improvisation: verbatim from official Trinity tables [T] (read from the PDF text and page images). Playback/improvising columns verbatim from official tables.
- RSL technical exercises, tempi, sight-reading, ear-test content, piece maxima: verbatim from official RSL spec [R].
- Gaps: the G2 time-signature entry is printed as 2/4 and looks inconsistent (unknown intent). Trinity gives no keys, no note counts per bar, and no fill-density figures; RSL gives no time-signature list per grade. The statement "drop one ceiling per step" is my inference, not board text. ABRSM drum-kit equivalent not found. Song-level groove vocabulary (e.g. which beats the kick may hit) is not published, so arranging below ceiling level is judgment.
