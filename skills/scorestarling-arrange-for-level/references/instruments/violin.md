# Violin: difficulty by level (ABRSM)

**Read this when** the target instrument is violin, at any level. Numbers come from ABRSM Bowed Strings syllabus from 2024; the checker reads them from [../profiles/violin.json](../profiles/violin.json): `python3 scripts/check_level.py arrangement.musicxml --instrument violin --grade N`. S1, S2 … in the tables point to the Sources section at the end; the master list with a confidence note for each is [../sources.md](../sources.md). Compiled 2026-10-14.

Pitches are scientific pitch (middle C = C4). "SR" = the sight-reading test. Notation in cells: maj = major, min = minor, oct = octaves, q = crotchet, 3# = three sharps in the key signature.

## Table by grade

It is a specification for the unseen test, NOT a limit on what the three set pieces contain; ABRSM publishes no equivalent table for pieces. Other documented points (S1): SR gives 30 seconds to look through and try out any part; fingering printed on the test is only a guide; SR is worth 21 of 150 marks, scales 21, each piece 30, aural 18 (Pass 100). All scales are played from memory, from the lowest possible tonic, with even notes or "long tonic" (one long first note) at the candidate's choice. Initial-Grade-to-Grade-3 pieces may be duets (candidate plays the upper part) and some set pieces are unaccompanied; list A pieces are generally faster and technical, B lyrical, C varied in tradition.

## What changes between neighbouring grades

- Initial to 1: E string joins (top D5 to A5), 3/4, minims and four-quaver groups, f and p. No new SR keys. Slurred scales (2 quavers to a bow) start in technical work.
- 1 to 2: low G string joins (bottom D4 to G3); G maj and e min; dotted minims, longer rests; first slurs in SR (two notes); mp and hairpins; length doubles to 8 bars.
- 2 to 3: first flat keys (F, B-flat) and A/C/D/G minors with accidentals; dotted rhythms, semiquaver patterns, ties, staccato, pizzicato; chromatic scale appears; scales begin to exceed 1st position (D6).
- 3 to 4: the largest step in the SR table: shifts 1st to 3rd, 6/8, anacrusis, chromatic notes, hooked bows, accents/tenuto, pauses, pp and ff; SR range to D6; dominant sevenths.
- 4 to 5: SR doubles in length (to 8-16 bars), shifts "as required", syncopation, arco/pizzicato changes, rit., simple chords; keys to 4#/4b; first 3-octave scales; diminished sevenths.
- 5 to 6: new time signatures 9/8, 5/8, 5/4 and triplets; a tempo; no SR range growth; double-stop scales (broken sixths) enter.
- 6 to 7: 7/8 and 7/4, left-hand pizzicato, SR to G6, 16-20 bars; broken octaves; 3-oct scales in A, B, D.
- 7 to 8: 12/8, ornaments, acceleration, 8va signs, SR to A6; every scale 3 octaves; parallel octaves and sixths, broken thirds.

## Arranging implications

Official limits (from S1) are marked [O]; my inferences are marked [I].
- Key: choose a key from the cumulative SR key list for the target grade [O]. Initial-G1: D or A major only; G2: add G, e; G3: add C, F, B-flat, a, d, g; G4: E-flat; G5: E, A-flat, b, c; G6: c#; G7: f#; G8: B, D-flat, f. Transpose the melody to the nearest listed key rather than leaving it in a remote key. Minor keys: none in SR before G2, natural form only at G2, any form from G3 [O].
- Range: keep every note inside the SR range for the grade [O]: Initial D4-G4 and A4-D5; G1 D4-A5; G2 G3-A5; G3 G3-B5; G4 G3-D6; G5-G6 G3-E6; G7 G3-G6; G8 G3-A6. To go down a grade, octave-displace the highest phrases down first. [I] Pieces may touch a little beyond the SR range; the scale ceilings (A5, A5, B-flat5, D6, E6, A6, B-flat6, D7, E7 for Initial-G8) are the official upper bound of what a candidate at that grade drills, so treat them as a hard stop.
- Rhythm: strip the feature introduced at the grade above [O]. Initial: only crotchets, paired quavers, crotchet rests; G1: add minims and four-quaver groups; G2: dotted minim; G3: dotted crotchet-quaver, dotted quaver-semiquaver, ties, simple semiquavers; G4: anacrusis; G5: simple syncopation; G6: triplets. Avoid 6/8 below G4, and 9/8, 5/8, 5/4 (G6), 7/8, 7/4 (G7), 12/8 (G8) below those grades.
- Bowing: Initial and G1 write every note detached (no slurs) [O]; G2 allow two-note slurs [O]; hooked bowing only from G4 [O]. For longer slurs [I]: technical work uses 2 quavers to a bow from G1 and 3 notes to a bow for arpeggios from G3, so 3-note slurs are defensible at G3 and 4-note slurs at G4; do not slur more than that below G6.
- Articulation: staccato and pizzicato from G3; accents and tenuto from G4; left-hand pizzicato only G7+ [O].
- Dynamics: Initial mf only; G1 f, p; G2 mp and hairpins; G4 pp, ff [O]. Remove extreme dynamics when simplifying.
- Accidentals: none foreign to the key until G3 (minor-key accidentals only); free chromatic notes from G4 [O]. Remove passing chromatics to drop below G4.
- Positions: no shifts below G4 [O]; at G4 only 1st to 3rd; from G5 shifts "as required" [O]. [I] Re-register or re-voice any line that leaves 1st position to stay at G3 or below; D6 is the first note in the technical list that needs a shift (G3).
- Chords and double stops: simple chords only at the very end of an SR from G5 [O]; broken-step double stops G6+, parallel octaves and sixths only G8 [O]. [I] Do not write double stops in a melody arranged below G5; for G5-G6 limit to an end chord.
- Vibrato: ABRSM says pieces heavily reliant on vibrato tend not to appear before about Grade 5 [O]. Do not make an Initial-G4 arrangement depend on vibrato for effect.
- Length and phrasing: SR lengths are 4 to 8 bars at Initial-G3 and up to 16-24 bars at G7-G8 [O]; set pieces are far longer and ABRSM does not publish their length. [I] Cut or repeat-collapse to keep the part short at low grades. ABRSM tells candidates to follow da capo/dal segno but skip other repeats unless very short, so avoid repeat structures that need first/second endings at low grades.
- Tempo: the official scale speeds (crotchet 46 at Initial rising to 120 at G8) are the only tempo data ABRSM gives [O]; [I] use them as a rough ceiling for sustained running notes, and slow the arrangement tempo before simplifying notes.
- Going down one grade, in order: (1) transpose to a lower-grade key, (2) pull notes inside the lower SR range, (3) remove the newest rhythm and articulation items listed above, (4) delete shifts, (5) drop dynamic markings above that grade's set.

## Confidence and gaps

- Keys: SR new-key lists verbatim from S1 (HIGH); cumulative reading follows ABRSM's statement that parameters are cumulative, but the cumulative key set is my assembly. Technical-work keys verbatim from S1 (HIGH). Keys actually used in set pieces: unknown (no official data; repertoire lists not analysed).
- Time signatures: SR list verbatim (HIGH). Initial row shows two lines (4 bars with 4/4, 6 bars with 2/4); that pairing is how I read the rendered table (MEDIUM). Grade 1 "3/4" with no other signature shown may mean only 3/4 is added; Grades 2-3 and 5 show none new. Time signatures in set pieces: unknown.
- Note values and rhythms: SR verbatim, but several symbols (beamed quaver groups, the rest at Grade 2, dotted shapes at Grade 3) were read from images; my verbal renderings are MEDIUM-HIGH. Rhythm in set pieces: unknown.
- Range: SR ranges verbatim from S1 in Helmholtz notation; conversion to scientific pitch is mine (HIGH after checking against the printed stave). Scale ceilings are computed by me from official requirement lists (HIGH arithmetic, assumes "lowest possible tonic"; open-string starts at Initial-G1 used as stated). Typical range of the set pieces: UNKNOWN; ABRSM does not publish it and the Pathways document has none.
- Positions: official only for SR (1st position at Initial, shifts 1st-3rd at G4, "as required" at G5). The statements that scales need a shift at G3 and positions "5th-7th" at G5 are derived from note spans and standard violin geometry, not from ABRSM (MEDIUM). Positions in set pieces: unknown/inferred.
- Bowing/articulation: scale bowing and slur counts verbatim (HIGH); SR articulation verbatim (HIGH); anything about piece bowing is inferred.
- Dynamics: SR verbatim (HIGH); piece dynamics unknown.
- SR length: verbatim (HIGH); "c." means approximate in the original. No tempo is specified for SR.
- Scale speeds: verbatim from S1 p. 18 (HIGH); described by ABRSM as a general guide.
- Secondary sources add nothing quantitative; the profile rests on S1 (cross-checked against S2). The biggest gap is piece-level characteristics (range, positions, key, rhythm in set pieces), which is the exact thing an arranger most wants and which no free public source supplies. Fill it by sampling the published exam-piece books per grade if available.
