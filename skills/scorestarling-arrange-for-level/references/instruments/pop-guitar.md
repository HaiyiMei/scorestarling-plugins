## What changes between neighbouring grades

- Initial to 1: tempo to 160, more full chords and melodic bits; staccato, accents.
- 1 to 2: 3-note power chords, 7th chords (min7, maj7), riffs and written solos, damping/palm mute/slides/hammer-on, first overdrive.
- 2 to 3: 6/8 and 12/8, semiquavers, half/full barre chords, bends, vibrato, fingerstyle, up-the-neck positions, 2-octave scales.
- 3 to 4: all regular meters, full barre chords as standard, heavy distortion/clean switching, faster shifts, 4-bar solos, maj7/min7 voicings.
- 4 to 5: any key, regular syncopation, irregular meters appear, effects and bends sophistication, 8-bar solos, positions to 5th.
- 5 to 6: complex syncopation, slow-tempo phrasing, harmonics, modal scales, tapping/legato in style studies, positions to 7th, 12-bar solos.
- 6 to 7: odd meters (7/8, 5/4) in tests, 9th chords, full fretboard, 16-bar solos. 7 to 8: altered chords/scales, any technique.

## Arranging implications

- Chord vocabulary ladder (RSL + Trinity): open major/minor triads and 2-note power chords (Initial-G1); 3-note power chords, m7/maj7 open chords (G2); half/full barre and dom7 (G3); maj7/min7/dom7 voicings on E and A strings (G4); triads in inversions (G5); m7b5, diminished (G6); 9ths (G7); altered 7ths (G8).
- Keys used in tests widen from guitar-friendly D, C, G, A major and E, A, D minor at Initial-G2, to F major and B minor at G3, E/A major and C minor at G4, E/B major and G/F minor at G5, any key from G6 [T][R]. Keep sharps-heavy keys for power-chord parts before G3 (my inference).
- Meter/rhythm: 4/4, 2/4, 3/4 up to G2; compound (6/8, 12/8) from G3; all regular meters at G4; occasional odd meters at G5, frequent at G6+. Eighths and quarters only at Initial-G1; semiquavers occasional at G2, regular at G3; syncopation simple (Initial-G3), growing (G4), regular (G5), complex (G6+).
- Position: stay in open position at Initial-G1 (Trinity playback: within 1st position, no chords); 2nd position at G3; up to 5th at G5; up to 7th at G6; full fretboard at G7. TAB can supplement staff notation at every grade.
- Techniques to add or strip: palm mute/damping/slides from G2; hammer-on/pull-off from G2-G3; bends and vibrato from G3 (quarter/semi/whole-tone bends); distortion on/off switching and slurred octaves G4; mandolin-style picking G5; pinch harmonics, wah, phase G6; tapping/legato G6 (RSL style study); anything G8.
- Part roles: Initial-G1 mainly chordal; G2 add riffs and a written solo; G3 freer melodic writing; G5 fully varied (lead, riff, melody, texture); improvised solos 4 / 8 / 12 / 16 bars at G4 / G5 / G6 / G7.
- Length: one song 45 s-2 min at Initial rising to 3.5-5 min at G8 [T]; RSL piece maxima 1:00 (Debut) to 3:45 (G8). RSL riff exercises are 4 bars (Debut-G1) then 8 bars, moved to new roots: good template for riff parts.

## Confidence and gaps

- All table cells: verbatim from official Trinity [T] and RSL [R] PDFs (symbols such as time signatures read from page images). No secondary sources used.
- Range/position: official but coarse (Trinity gives positions in prose and, for playback, 1st/2nd/5th/7th/full; RSL gives scale root strings). No string-by-string or fret-by-fret limits per grade, no written-pitch range: unknown, so the 1st/2nd/5th/7th position ceilings (playback tests) are the best proxy.
- Trinity G2 time signature printed as 2/4 (likely misprint; intent unknown); "ring" under G2 techniques is printed as is (probably let-ring; unknown).
- Gaps: no numeric chord-change rates, no bars-per-song figures, no list of technique counts per bar. RSL spec is the 2018 edition (a 2024 RSL guitar book exists but its specification was not retrieved).
